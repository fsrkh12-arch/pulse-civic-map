# PULSE — Civic Map

[Open the PULSE prototype](https://fsrkh12-arch.github.io/pulse-civic-map/)

PULSE explores a better way for residents of Baku to make local concerns visible to public agencies. It brings reports of road hazards, damaged infrastructure, crowd conditions, and community events together on a map.

## The idea

The long-term goal is to use AI to categorize reports, assess their urgency and how many people have reported them, and help route the details to the appropriate agency. We are pitching the concept to government stakeholders.

A potential future integration is [SİMA](https://sima.az/?lang=en), an Azerbaijani digital platform that provides identity verification and digital signatures.

## The prototype

Explore an interactive map, search and filter illustrative reports, add a report, and try the location and community confirmation features.

PULSE is an early prototype: reports and confirmations reset when the page reloads and are not shared with other users. The AI, agency routing, and SİMA integration are future plans. PULSE is not currently affiliated or integrated with a government agency and does not contact emergency services.
